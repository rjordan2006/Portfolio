Leadership

My Philosophy

Great products don't happen by accident. They emerge from the right culture, the right process, and leaders who empower their teams to act like owners.

Outcomes Over Outputs
Why do so many product teams ship features, and yet don't move the needle? The work I'm most proud of has always started with a clear "why" — connecting every initiative directly to revenue impact, customer value, and outcomes that actually matter to the business.
Global Team Builder
Some of my best work has been done across ten time zones and three continents. Distributed teams can outperform co-located ones when leaders invest in trust, clarity, and genuine care for each person's growth. Every member of my team deserves to know where they're headed — and to know I'm invested in getting them there.
AI-Forward Leadership
I was an early adopter, and I'm not slowing down. AI is reshaping what great product management looks like, and I coach my teams to lean into that shift — using it for market analysis, prototyping, and strategic thinking rather than treating it as a shortcut.

Commercial Accountability
I transform product managers who optimize for features or satisfaction scores in isolation, to strategic product leaders who understand the business model, own their numbers, and make decisions with accountability.
Transformation Specialist
Change is not something that happens to great product organizations — it's something they pursue deliberately. I've led transformations across methodology, structure, and technology: from BDD adoption and shift-left PDLCs to post-acquisition integrations and platform migrations. I know how to take an organization through hard change and come out stronger.
Cross-Functional Partnership
The best product outcomes I've been part of were never the result of product teams working in isolation. They happened at the intersection of engineering, sales, marketing, and customer success — I build real bridges, not just strategy decks.
